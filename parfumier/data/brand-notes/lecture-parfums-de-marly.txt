Parfums de Marly | Althaïr Exclusif || Althair Exclusif composes an olfactory sonata built around an intense Bourbon vanilla, elevated by the velvety nuances of amaretto and the warm radiance of saffron. Addictive, refined and captivating.
Parfums de Marly | Pegasus Exclusif || Pegasus Exclusif opens with vibrant accents of pink pepper, unfolds into the delicate smoothness of almond, then deepens into a woody trail of oud and vanilla. Powerful, charismatic and assertive.
Parfums de Marly | Perseus || Perseus combines bright grapefruit, fresh geranium, and refined vetiver. Bright, invigorating and refined.
Parfums de Marly | Galloway || Galloway reveals bright, zesty notes of lemon blended with spicy freshness. It rests on a warm base of ambrox and musks. Elegant, pure and bright.
Parfums de Marly | Kalan || Kalan fuses zesty orange with black pepper notes and aromatic lavender, grounded by warm, radiant cedarwood. Fiery, luxurious and splendid.
Parfums de Marly | Athalia || Athalia opens with a bold bitter orange, followed by a warm heart of iris and cashmeran, and settling into a rich base of musks and amber. Mysterious, enigmatic and captivating.
Parfums de Marly | Godolphin || Godolphin highlights warm accents of saffron, reveals the power of noble leather, and unfolds into an intense amber base. Unnbound, vigorous and sophisticated.
Parfums de Marly | Cassili || Cassili opens with a delightful burst of peach, unveiling vibrant accents of plum and mimosa, and settling into a comforting trail of creamy sandalwood and vanilla. Joyful, soft and lively.
